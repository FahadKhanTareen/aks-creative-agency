Where we work / THE UAE

Projection mapping in the UAE

Building projection, LED facade content and immersive activations for venues, hotels, developers and event agencies in Dubai, Abu Dhabi and the other emirates.

AKS CREATIVE STUDIO← Services and regionsView projects ↗

The UAE has some of the most ambitious architecture and event calendars in the world, and a competitive production market to match. AKS Creative Studio works with event agencies, developers, hotels and brands there as a specialist content and projection design studio, one hour behind the UAE from Islamabad.

The studio was founded in 2019 by projection mapping artist Fahad Khan Tareen. His work took 2nd place at the World Championship of Projection Mapping on the TV Tower at the Festival of Lights in Berlin in 2019, created as lead artist with Artec, and the studio has since mapped Parliament House in Islamabad, Mohatta Palace in Karachi, Minar-e-Pakistan in Lahore and the Pakistan Monument.

What the studio delivers

  • Projection mapping on buildings, monuments and stages, from concept to live show.
  • Content for LED facades and media walls already installed at the venue.
  • VR, AR and HoloFan activations for launches and exhibitions.
  • Motion design and brand films, produced remotely.

Occasions that suit projection mapping

National Day on 2 December and the weeks around it, New Year, Ramadan and Eid campaigns, hotel and tower openings, property launches, mall activations and corporate gala nights. Many UAE venues also have permanent LED facades that need fresh content for each of these moments.

How remote and on-site delivery works

Most of the work happens before anyone travels. Concept, content, projection design and previsualisation are produced from the studio, with review calls on your schedule. The studio then travels for the site survey if one is needed and for show week: rigging with the local production company, alignment, rehearsal and running the show. Projectors, rigging and power are normally hired from a supplier near the venue, which keeps freight and customs out of the budget. If you already have an event production company, the studio works alongside them.

Site conditions to plan for

Heat and dust affect equipment more than rain does. Projectors need shade, airflow and filters cleaned during long runs, and outdoor positions need protection from sand. Sunset is early in winter, which helps: shows can start at a family-friendly hour. Light from surrounding towers and roads is the other factor, so a site visit at night is worth more than daytime photos. See weather-proofing outdoor projection mapping.

Permissions

Public shows usually need agreement from the building owner, the local authority or event regulator, and the people responsible for crowd safety and power. Start this early; permissions for public projection mapping shows lists who usually has to approve what. A local event partner normally handles the applications.

How much does a projection mapping show cost?

It depends mainly on facade size, show length, number of nights and how bright the site is. What drives the cost of a projection mapping show explains each factor. Send the brief and you will get a range early.

How far ahead should we start?

For a new building, allow two to three months from brief to show night. Short shows on simple surfaces can be faster if the venue has drawings.

Do you have a team in the UAE?

The studio is based in Islamabad and travels for survey and show week. Equipment and crew are hired locally, often through your event production company.
