ABOUT

The artist behind the light

Fahad Khan Tareen is a projection mapping artist and immersive experience designer working at the intersection of architecture, motion and light. For seven years his work has turned static surfaces (national monuments, palaces, forts, hotel and mall facades) into moving canvases, run live in front of a crowd.

His projection mapping has run on Pakistan's own monuments (Minar-e-Pakistan, Parliament House, Mohatta Palace, Rohtas Fort) and on the international stage, where a 2019 entry took 2nd place at the World Championship of Projection Mapping in Berlin, followed by the BBBank Newcomer award in Karlsruhe in 2021.

The practice spans architectural projection mapping, interactive installations, VR / AR, and AI-assisted content. Built the same way every time: connect on the constraints, concept the idea, then build and run it on site until it works exactly as drawn.
