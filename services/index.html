Services

  • Architectural projection mapping: Architectural projection mapping for monuments, heritage buildings, hotels and brand launches: survey, content, projector design and live show operation.
  • Content for LED facades and media walls: Content for LED facades, media walls and large screens, designed to the pixel map and viewing distance so it reads from the street and loops well.
  • VR and AR activations: Virtual reality walkthroughs and augmented reality activations for property sales, exhibitions and brand events, plus HoloFan and mesh displays.
  • Motion design and brand films: 2D and 3D motion graphics, brand films, title sequences, loops and short-form social content from a projection mapping studio, delivered remotely.

The studio was founded in 2019 by projection mapping artist Fahad Khan Tareen. His work took 2nd place at the World Championship of Projection Mapping on the TV Tower at the Festival of Lights in Berlin in 2019, created as lead artist with Artec, and the studio has since mapped Parliament House in Islamabad, Mohatta Palace in Karachi, Minar-e-Pakistan in Lahore and the Pakistan Monument.

Where we work

The studio is based in Islamabad and works across Pakistan and internationally, remotely and on the ground.
