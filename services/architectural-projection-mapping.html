Architectural projection mapping

Content built to the exact shape of a building, then projected so the facade itself appears to move, open and change.

AKS CREATIVE STUDIO← Services and regionsView projects ↗

Architectural projection mapping turns a building into the screen. Instead of throwing a rectangle of video at a wall, the content is drawn to the facade's own windows, columns, arches and cornices, so light lands exactly where the architecture is. Done well, the audience stops seeing a projection and starts seeing the building do something it cannot do.

The studio was founded in 2019 by projection mapping artist Fahad Khan Tareen. His work took 2nd place at the World Championship of Projection Mapping on the TV Tower at the Festival of Lights in Berlin in 2019, created as lead artist with Artec, and the studio has since mapped Parliament House in Islamabad, Mohatta Palace in Karachi, Minar-e-Pakistan in Lahore and the Pakistan Monument.

What the work covers

  • Site visit and survey: drawings, measurements or a 3D scan of the facade, and where the audience and projectors can stand.
  • Concept: the story, mood and pacing, agreed with you before production starts.
  • Content production: 2D and 3D animation rendered to the facade template, with sound design.
  • Projection design: projector count, brightness, lenses, positions and overlap, so the image is bright and sharp across the whole surface.
  • Previsualisation: the show seen on a model of the building before anything is hired.
  • On site: rigging with the production crew, alignment, warping and blending, rehearsal and running the show live.

Where it works best

Monuments and heritage buildings, where the architecture already carries meaning. National days and anniversaries. Hotel, mall and tower facades for openings and seasonal campaigns. Brand and product launches that need one image people will photograph and share. Each needs a dark enough site, a clear audience area and somewhere safe to put the projectors.

How a project runs

Most shows follow the same order: brief and site details, a feasibility answer, a concept, a survey, content production with review rounds, previsualisation, then load-in, alignment and rehearsal in show week. Field Notes covers each stage in depth, starting with planning an architectural projection mapping show, surveying a building and the show-week schedule.

What drives the cost

Facade size and brightness of the site decide how many projectors are needed. Show length and complexity decide how much content has to be made. Number of nights, location, permissions and power add the rest. What drives the cost of a projection mapping show breaks this down, and what to send a studio before a quote lists the details that make a quote accurate.

How long does a projection mapping show take to produce?

A short show on a well-documented building can be produced in a few weeks. Larger facades, longer shows or sites that need a new survey usually need two to three months from brief to show night.

Can you work outside Pakistan?

Yes. Content, projection design and previsualisation are done remotely, and the studio travels for survey, alignment and show week. Equipment is usually hired from a supplier near the venue.

Do you supply the projectors?

Projectors are normally hired for the event from a rental company near the site. The studio specifies the kit, checks it on arrival and runs it.
