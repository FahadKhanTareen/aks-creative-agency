Motion design and brand films

2D and 3D animation, brand films, titles and loops, produced remotely for agencies, brands and event companies.

Projection mapping content is motion design under hard conditions: huge canvases, odd shapes, audiences at a distance and no second take on show night. The same team makes motion work for normal screens too, for agencies and brands that need a studio which can carry a piece from idea to final render.

What the work covers

  • 2D and 3D motion graphics and explainers.
  • Brand films and promos.
  • Short-form social content in vertical and square formats.
  • Title sequences, stings and looping backgrounds for stages and screens.
  • Sound design to match.
  • AI-assisted production where it saves time without lowering quality, always labelled as such.

Working remotely

Motion projects run fully online: a brief and references, a style frame or animatic for approval, then production with agreed review rounds. Files are delivered in the formats your edit, broadcast or playback team needs. Agencies can white-label the work.

How a motion project runs

A brief with references and the deliverables list; a style frame or two to fix the look; an animatic or storyboard with timing; production; and review rounds agreed at the start, usually two. Final files come in every aspect ratio and format you need, named and versioned so nothing is lost between teams.

Cost depends mostly on length, whether the piece is 2D or 3D, how many versions and aspect ratios are needed, and how much of the material exists already, such as logos, product models or footage. A clear deliverables list at the start is the best way to keep a quote accurate.

Content for stages and events

Event screens are wider and taller than broadcast, and often split across several surfaces. Content for them is planned to the stage drawing and the media server, the same way as a mapping show. Designing projection content for a building facade and content delivery and version control explain the approach.

Do you work with agencies on a white-label basis?

Yes. Motion and content work can be delivered under the agency's name.

Which time zones do you work with?

The studio is in Islamabad (GMT+5) and works with clients in the Gulf, Europe and Southeast Asia, with review calls planned around both schedules.
